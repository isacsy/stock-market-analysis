## About Me

I'm a Finance undergraduate at **Universiti Tunku Abdul Rahman (UTAR)**, Kampar, Perak, Malaysia, originally from Sabah, with a strong pull toward the analytical side of finance — turning raw market and business data into decisions people can actually act on.

My work sits at the intersection of **financial analysis, data analytics, and business intelligence**. I'm comfortable moving a problem from a messy spreadsheet all the way to a polished, interactive dashboard: cleaning data, writing SQL to structure and query it, computing the financial metrics that matter (returns, volatility, moving averages), and visualizing the results so the story is obvious at a glance.

I care about **data-driven decision making** — using evidence rather than intuition — and about presenting analysis the way a stakeholder actually needs to see it: clear, contextualized, and honest about risk.

**Languages spoken:** English (Upper-Intermediate) · Chinese (Native) · Malay (Intermediate)

**Open To:**
- Internships in Financial Analysis, Data Analytics, or Business Intelligence
- Data-focused finance projects (equity research, risk analysis, portfolio analytics)
- Collaborations on data visualization and SQL-based reporting

## Featured Projects

<details>
<summary><b>📊 Apple Inc. (AAPL) Stock Performance & Risk Analysis</b></summary>
<br/>

Analysis of Apple Inc. (AAPL) stock performance over a one-year period (Apr 2025 – Apr 2026), evaluating return, risk, and price trends to assess the stock's attractiveness as an investment. Raw daily closing price data was transformed into financial metrics and an interactive dashboard.

| Aspect | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Stack** | Microsoft Excel, MySQL, Tableau |
| **Scope** | 1-year daily closing price data (24 Apr 2025 – 24 Apr 2026) |
| **Metrics** | Daily return, average daily return, total return, volatility (std. dev.), 10-day moving average |
| **Key Finding** | ~30% total return, positive average daily return, moderate volatility |
| **Impact** | Interactive Tableau dashboard for at-a-glance investment insight |
| **Repository** | [isacsy/stock-market-analysis](https://github.com/isacsy/stock-market-analysis) |

Data was sourced from Yahoo Finance and Investing.com, cleaned in Excel, stored and queried in MySQL, and visualized in an interactive Tableau dashboard. The project demonstrates an end-to-end financial analysis workflow — from raw price data to actionable, stakeholder-ready insight.

**[View the Interactive Dashboard →](https://public.tableau.com/shared/P2NKG5J54?:display_count=n&:origin=viz_share_link)**
